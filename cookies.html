Cookie & Tracking Policy

1. Definition & Purpose of Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when navigating modern websites.
They serve to store user preferences, manage secure session states, and compile aggregated usage statistics.
BrightStudyGo utilizes cookies to remember font scaling, layout settings, and active session configurations.
These files do not contain malware, executable code, or malicious software scripts designed to harm devices.
You can inspect, clear, or restrict cookies through browser preferences at any moment during your session.
For physical correspondence regarding cookies, write to 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.

2. Categories of Cookies Deployed

Essential cookies are strictly necessary for core navigation, server load balancing, and security authentication.
Performance cookies collect anonymous data regarding page load speeds, error logs, and visitor navigation routes.
Functional cookies store your personalized interactive settings, such as technique selector and quiz state.
Analytics cookies powered by Google Tag (gtag.js) measure audience retention and article engagement metrics.
We do not deploy invasive third-party ad retargeting trackers or unauthorized data harvesting scripts.
Questions concerning cookie classification can be directed to our technical hotline at +1-888-777-5845.

3. Google Analytics Integration Details

Our platform operates Google Tag analytics software under the official site tag property G-0LY0HY7L01.
Google Analytics uses persistent performance cookies to track how visitors move through our study essays.
Data gathered includes browser types, referral URLs, screen resolutions, and country-level geographic location.
This aggregate information enables our academic desk team to curate study guides aligned with reader preferences.
All IP addresses processed by Google Tag tools on BrightStudyGo are masked to protect individual identity.
You may review Google's official privacy and cookie documentation for comprehensive technical details.

4. Managing & Disabling Cookies

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can alter browser settings to block or delete them.
Modifying cookie permissions may disable specific interactive features on BrightStudyGo, such as study quizzes.
To manage cookie controls in Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge, visit settings.
You can also install dedicated browser extensions designed to block web analytics scripts across all sites.
Disabling cookies will not prevent you from accessing our core study masterclasses and published articles.
